Can I order the door without installation?

Yes. Let us know whether you need the door only or would like to discuss installation. Include your town so we can confirm delivery or pickup arrangements and installation availability.

What measurements should I send?

Start with the opening width and height, nearby wall space and available height above the opening. These are preliminary measurements; track requirements and final door dimensions still need confirmation.
