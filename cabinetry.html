Questions before we begin

Can you build to fit an existing space?

Yes. Built-ins and specialty pieces start with the space and the work you need them to do. Final dimensions require a site review.

Do I need a finished design?

No. Bring your ideas, approximate measurements and examples of styles you like. We can work through the layout and selections together.

Where do you work?

We are based in Philadelphia, Mississippi. Include your town with your inquiry so we can confirm service and installation availability.

Let’s start with your space.

Tell us what you have in mind, your town and your approximate dimensions. We’ll review your project and discuss the next steps. Measurements and selections must be confirmed before final pricing.
